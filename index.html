Every stitch has a job

Eight construction decisions, each made for hot, humid field conditions. Nothing decorative. Nothing standard-issue.

Mesh heat zones

Field questions

Before you order

Which size should I get?

M fits most UK 6–9 (EU 39–43); L fits UK 9–12 (EU 43–47). If you're between sizes and wear thicker boots, size up.

Are these allowed with uniform?

They're plain black, boot height, with no visible branding when worn — cut to sit cleanly with standard combat boots. Always check your unit's dress requirements.

How do I wash them?

Cold machine wash, inside out, hang dry. Skip the dryer and bleach — that's what breaks down performance fibres and elastic in any sock.

Where do you ship?

Orders are fulfilled through our Shopee store with standard Singapore-wide delivery options at checkout.
